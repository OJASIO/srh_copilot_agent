## Semester ticket: Berlin, Dresden, Hamburg, Leipzig

At these campuses the Deutschlandsemesterticket is compulsory. It is valid from 1 October to 31 March
(winter semester) and 1 April to 30 September (summer semester), nationwide on local and regional public
transport, 2nd class only. Always carry your student ID and an official photo ID. You must activate the
ticket at the start of each new semester.

Price: 208.80 euros in the winter semester 2025/26 and the summer semester 2026. The price for the winter
semester 2026/27 was not yet known on 31 March 2026.

Payment: with a SEPA mandate the ticket is collected together with the tuition fees; if you pay the fees
yourself, transfer the ticket fee as well (payment deadline in your study contract).

Opting out: possible only with proof, for a semester abroad (study or internship) or a stay abroad of at
least three consecutive months. The deadline is announced before each semester, usually up to 4 weeks
before the semester starts. During a leave semester you have no ticket and do not need to opt out.

Leipzig: install the LeipzigMOVE app, register with your university email ("Fuer L-Login registrieren"),
and add the ticket under "Abos & Buchungen", "Bestehendes D-Ticket hinzufuegen" with the contract number
LVB sends you shortly before the semester. Ticket problems in Leipzig: Leipziger Verkehrsbetriebe,
https://www.l.de/verkehrsbetriebe/kontakt/

## Semester ticket: Heidelberg, Stuttgart, Karlsruhe, Tuebingen

There is no semester ticket at these campuses. Students under 27 can buy the D-Ticket JugendBW
(Deutschlandticket for young people in Baden-Wuerttemberg, 39 euros per month on 31 March 2026) from the
VRN or Deutsche Bahn.

## Health insurance notification (ESMV / M10)

Student Service can check whether your insurer's digital notification (ESMV) has arrived. If it has not,
ask your insurer to send it to SRH University Heidelberg with the university number H0001444, whatever
your campus is.
If you have private insurance or European insurance (EHIC), or you are over 30 and exempt from compulsory
insurance, an electronic exemption notification (M10-2) must still be sent. Any statutory insurer creates
it on request with proof of the exemption reason; your private insurer informs a statutory insurer, which
then sends the M10-2 to SRH.

## Login details

Login details are sent after you are fully enrolled (all conditions in the application portal met) and
your health insurance notification has arrived. Data is transferred to the student system every working
day around noon; if your enrolment is completed later in the day, it is transferred on the next working
day. Transfers for a new semester start 6 weeks before the semester.

## Cancelling your study contract (Kuendigung)

Send the cancellation to Fee Management of your campus: in writing by post to your campus, in person, or
by email from your SRH address with the signed cancellation letter as PDF. The notice period depends on
your contract date and contract version; ask Fee Management for your individual period.

## Student ID

From the winter semester 2025/26 student IDs are digital and no longer issued as cards (except for exchange
students and SRH International College). The digital ID is valid together with your ID card, passport or
residence permit, because it has no photo. It is valid from the first day of the semester (1 April or
1 October) until the end of the semester (31 March or 30 September). Download it in CampusWeb under
"My documents", in the drop-down list (like the enrolment certificate).
Lost physical student ID: in Heidelberg report it to the smartcard office (Maria-Probst-Str. 3); at all
other campuses report it to Student Service. A new ID costs 5 euros.

## Leave of absence: quick answers

Apply to Student Service by 1 March (summer semester) or 1 September (winter semester). Not possible for an
Erasmus semester abroad or during a mandatory internship. The full process is in the separate document on
leave of absence.

## IT problems

Berlin, Dresden, Hamburg, Leipzig: first email Student Service with a precise description and screenshots;
they check common causes such as a block or a missing health insurance notification. If they cannot help,
CampusWeb, CampusNet and Microsoft 365 issues go to itdesk.hsbe@srh.de, eCampus issues to
ecampus-support.hsg@srh.de.
Heidelberg: contact Student Service for blocks, missing health insurance notification or payment delays;
for CampusNet, CampusWeb and Microsoft 365 go to the IT consultation hour in the foyer of the blue tower,
Monday to Thursday 13:00 to 14:00; for Moodle (eCampus) write to ecampus-support.hsg@srh.de.

## Complaints

Complaints are collected centrally at getbetter.hsg@srh.de and passed to the responsible teams (announced
on eCampus on 29.10.2025). "Anonymous" means the rectorate withholds your details on request; you still
write by email, so you are identifiable to the getbetter team.

## Re-entry after leaving (Wiedereinstieg)

To return to your studies you must apply again through the application portal (DreamApply). Only this
ensures you are enrolled in an accredited programme. Send your transcript of records as well, so programme
management and course coordination can place you in the right cohort.

## SRH International College

SRH International College (operated with Navitas) offers a foundation year to obtain the university
entrance qualification, currently in Heidelberg and Berlin. Contacts: support.heidelberg@srh-international-
college.de and support.berlin@srh-international-college.de. Participants need health insurance, receive
smart cards or student IDs, but no portal logins and no semester ticket.
