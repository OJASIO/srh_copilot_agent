# Leave of absence (Beurlaubung / Urlaubssemester): process for students

Source: SRH process description "Prozesssteckbrief Beurlaubung" (Student Service, Stand Januar 2026) and
the Student Service hotline FAQ (Stand 31.03.2026), section 10.5. Student-relevant steps only.
Responsible: Student Service, student-service.hsg@srh.de.

## Deadlines and how to apply

A leave of absence (Urlaubssemester) is requested with the official application form for academic leave,
submitted to Student Service together with proof of the reason (Nachweise).
Deadlines: 1 March for the summer semester, 1 September for the winter semester.

## What happens after you apply

1. Student Service checks the application formally, within at most 2 working days.
2. If the proof is not sufficient or information is missing, Student Service contacts you and you revise
   and resubmit the application within the deadline.
3. If the reason for the leave is not covered by the admission and enrolment regulations (ZulImO), or is
   not proven, the application is rejected and you are informed.
4. If approved, the leave is recorded in the system. Programme management decides which cohort or semester
   you join after the leave (within 2 working days), and course management sets your examination
   regulations and semester (within 2 working days).
5. Fee Management cancels the tuition fees for the semester of leave (within 2 working days). Special
   cases, for example open payments, are clarified between Fee Management and Student Service.
6. You and the services involved are informed of the approval.
7. In the last week before the leave semester your status is set to "on leave" (beurlaubt).

## Before you return

Course management (LVM) asks you to get in touch before you return from the leave, so that you are placed
in the right planning group.
During the leave: the Examination Office deregisters you from registered resit exams, Career Service &
Development checks and if needed pauses your funding status, and the Centre for Languages deregisters you
from language courses.

## When leave is not possible

- Not for an Erasmus semester abroad: a semester at a partner university requires that tuition fees are
  paid and university services can be used during that time.
- Not during a mandatory internship (Pflichtpraktikum), because the internship is part of the studies.
