# Student Service: services and "Good to know" for students

Sources: SRH University website, pages "Student Service"
(https://www.srh-university.de/en/your-studies/student-service/) and "Good to know"
(https://www.srh-university.de/en/your-studies/student-service/good-to-know/), retrieved 27 September 2026.

## Services and contact

Student Service supports students with: general questions during studies; enrolment and start of studies;
the student ID; formalities such as enrolment certificates and application forms; BAfoeG, residence
registration and accommodation information; health insurance advice; login credentials for the university
platforms. It also helps you find the right contact person for any request.

Contact: student-service.hsg@srh.de, phone +49 30 515650 200. Available on weekdays from 9 am to 3 pm
(Fridays until noon). Requests by email are processed within 72 hours.

## Start of studies: login details and enrolment certificate

Once all enrolment requirements are met, you receive log-in credentials for the university portals, where
you find all information for your studies. Before the course starts there is an information session; you
are invited by email in good time.

Login details are sent to your private email address about four weeks before the start of your studies,
provided all enrolment requirements are met:
1. You have arranged your health insurance.
2. You have paid your fees according to your study contract.
3. If you are from a non-European country: you have submitted confirmation of your visa or visa appointment.

Your enrolment certificate comes with the login details, about 4 weeks before the start. You can download
it yourself from CampusWeb once all admission criteria are met.
Delayed visa: contact your Study Advisor, who can advise you on your personal situation.

## Health insurance

Under the German Social Security Act, every student at a state or state-recognised university must prove
sufficient health insurance for the entire study period.

German statutory health insurance: tell your insurance company that you are enrolled at SRH University
(university number H0001444). The insurer sends SRH an ESMV, the digital confirmation of your insurance.

Private or European insurance (EHIC): apply to a German statutory health insurance company of your choice
for exemption from compulsory statutory insurance. The insurer needs: surname and first name, date of birth,
address, email address, copy of your ID or passport, copy of your letter of admission, the university name
(SRH University, H0001444), and a copy of your private insurance or EHIC card.

You do not submit proof yourself: a German statutory insurer must confirm your insurance to SRH digitally
(ESMV). This must arrive by the first day of your studies (1 April or 1 October) at the latest.

## Semester ticket

At some SRH campuses there is a Deutschlandsemesterticket; details are on eCampus. It is valid on all
2nd-class public transport throughout Germany, it is digital, and you need a photo ID together with your
student ID to use it.

## Other practical questions

- Timetable: on CampusWeb.
- Fees: contact Fee Management of your campus.
- Lost transponder or smartcard: contact Student Service, which issues a new card or transponder for a fee
  or refers you to the right service.
- Personal data: you must keep it up to date. Change it yourself on CampusWeb under "My profile"; for other
  changes email Student Service.
- Academic calendar: an overview of the semester structure is published on the Good to know page
  (Academic Calendar 2025 to 2030).
- Accommodation: the campus pages for Berlin, Dresden, Gera, Heidelberg and Leipzig have accommodation
  guidance.
