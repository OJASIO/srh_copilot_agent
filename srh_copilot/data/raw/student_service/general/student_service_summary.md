## What Student Service does
The Student & Welcome Service is the first point of contact for students: finding the right contact person, finding a room, help of any kind.

Responsibilities:
- General support during student and university life
- General paperwork: enrolment certificates, digital student ID, BAföG forms, application forms
- Information on: registration of address, accommodation, campus map
- Guidance on: health insurance, basic IT support
- Deutschlandsemesterticket information: https://ecampus.srh-university.de/mod/page/view.php?id=110538
- Contact point in case of accidents or minor injuries (first aid kit)

## Forms
- Application forms course on eCampus: https://ecampus.srh-university.de/course/view.php?id=11498&lang=en
- Application for Academic Leave (Beurlaubung): deadline 1 March for the summer semester and 1 September for the winter semester, submitted via CampusWeb to Student Service
- Application for Change of Study Programme or Campus: deadline 28 February (summer semester) or 31 August (winter semester) from the 2nd semester; at the start of studies an informal request by e-mail until 30 April (summer) or 31 October (winter)
- Application for Re-entry: following soon
