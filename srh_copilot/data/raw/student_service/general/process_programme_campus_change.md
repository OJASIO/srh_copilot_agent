# Changing study programme or campus: process for students

Source: SRH process descriptions "Prozesssteckbrief Studiengangs-/Standortwechsel bei Studienstart" and
"Prozesssteckbrief Studiengangs-/Standortwechsel ab dem 2. Semester" (Student Service, Stand Januar 2026).
Student-relevant steps only. Responsible: Student Service, student-service.hsg@srh.de.

## Two different processes

Which process applies depends on when you want to change:
- At the start of your studies (before the start or shortly after): an informal request, handled by
  Admission.
- From the 2nd semester onwards: a formal application with your transcript, handled by programme
  management, Admission and the Examination Office.

## Change at the start of studies

Deadline: 30 April for the summer semester, 31 October for the winter semester.
1. Send an informal written request (by email) to Student Service. Student Service forwards it to
   Admission (apply.hsg@srh.de).
2. Within at most 2 working days Admission checks whether the change is possible: admission requirements
   and whether the programme is offered.
3. If it is not possible, Admission informs you (Student Service in copy).
4. If it is possible, Admission sends you an additional agreement (Zusatzvereinbarung), backdated to the
   start of the semester. Sign it and return it to Admission within 2 working days.
5. Admission confirms receipt, tells you when the change takes effect in the system, and updates your data.
6. Student Service deactivates the old programme once the new one is set up, and informs Fee Management and
   course management. Fee Management cancels the old fees, sets the new fees and informs you about the new
   fee collection and any fee changes. The Examination Office deregisters you from the old programme's exams
   and registers you for the new ones.

## Change from the 2nd semester

Deadline: 28 February for the summer semester, 31 August for the winter semester.
Documents: the application form for a change of study programme or of campus, and your transcript of
records. Submit them by email to Student Service.
1. Student Service checks the application formally and forwards it to the programme management of the new
   programme.
2. Within at most 2 working days, programme management checks whether the change is possible and the
   desired cohort is offered, and whether modules can be transferred or credits need to be recognised.
3. If the change is not possible, you are informed with the reasons.
4. Transfer of modules or recognition of credits is handled within at most 5 working days each; with
   recognition, the Examination Office processes it.
5. Student Service asks Admission (apply.hsg@srh.de) about admission. Admission checks the admission
   requirements within 2 working days. If they are not met, the application is rejected with reasons.
6. If they are met, Admission prepares an additional agreement (Zusatzvereinbarung) with the credit
   recognition and the new fees. Student Service sends it to you; sign and return it within 2 working days.
7. Student Service confirms receipt and tells you when the change takes effect. After the end of the
   semester the new programme is booked and the old one deactivated.
8. Before the new semester starts, course management changes your planning group, the Examination Office
   completes the credit recognition, and Fee Management adjusts your fees and informs you.
For a change of campus, the services at the new campus are informed.
