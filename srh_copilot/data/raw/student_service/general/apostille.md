# Apostille and certification of SRH documents

Sources: Ministry of Science, Research and Arts Baden-Wuerttemberg, page "Apostillen/Beglaubigung"
(https://mwk.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/service/apostillen/beglaubigung, retrieved 27 September 2026) and
the Student Service hotline FAQ (Stand 31.03.2026), section 12.

## Who issues apostilles for SRH documents

Since the merger, the Berlin Senate no longer issues apostilles for SRH documents, because SRH University
has been accredited in Baden-Wuerttemberg since 1 October 2024. Responsible now is the Ministry of Science,
Research and Arts Baden-Wuerttemberg (MWK), which handles documents of state and state-recognised
universities in Baden-Wuerttemberg.

## How to apply

Applications are handled by post only. Send a completed cover letter (the form "Antrag Apostille/
Legalisierung", a Word file on the ministry's page) together with the documents. The documents must carry
an original signature and the official seal or stamp of the university. You can email PDFs (maximum 5 MB)
in advance to have them checked.

Fee: 13.00 euros per document. Processing time: usually about five to six weeks after the documents arrive
at the ministry.
