# SRH scholarships and study financing: amounts, deadlines, eligibility

Source: SRH University public website, page "Financing"
(https://www.srh-university.de/en/you-want-to-study/financing/) and page "Deutschlandstipendium"
(https://www.srh-university.de/en/study-at-srh/financing-your-studies/scholarships/deutschlandstipendium/).
Retrieved 27 September 2026. Amounts and deadlines can change; the responsible office has the final word.

## SRH Scholarships: overview

SRH offers partial scholarships to financially support first-year students of SRH Bachelor's and
Master's programmes. The scholarships cover up to 50% of the tuition fees of the first year of study.
They work as a fee reduction for the first year.

To be considered, complete your study application by the deadline: 15 January for the April intake,
15 July for the October intake.

## SRH Scholarships: how to apply

The first step is taken by the student. The study advisor does not start the process.

1. If you meet the criteria of one of the SRH Scholarships, you contact your study advisor yourself.
2. After that you receive an invitation to apply for the scholarship.
3. You submit the scholarship application within 2 weeks of receiving that invitation.

You need a confirmed study offer from SRH.

Study advisors for prospective students: studienberatung.hsg@srh.de (German) or contact.hsg@srh.de
(English). Applicants who already have an application account write to Admission: apply.hsg@srh.de.
Admission is responsible for all SRH Scholarship categories.

## SRH Scholarships for management programmes

- Performance Scholarship: proof of outstanding school or academic performance, an academic IELTS with
  an overall score of 7.0, and a letter of motivation or a portfolio presentation.
- Entrepreneurship Scholarship: proof of good school or academic performance, an academic IELTS with an
  overall score of 6.5, and a short conceptual presentation (5 slides).
- Women for Leadership Scholarship: proof of good school or academic performance, an academic IELTS with
  an overall score of 6.5, and a letter of motivation.

## SRH Scholarships for creative programmes

- Talent Scholarship: a letter of motivation introducing yourself and your passion, and at least three
  artistic work samples.
- Women in Sound Scholarship: an artistic portfolio.

## SRH Scholarships for technology programmes

- Future of Tech Scholarship: proof of good academic performance, an academic IELTS with an overall score
  of 6.5, a letter of motivation and a presentation (maximum 5 slides).
- Women in Tech Scholarship: proof of good academic performance, an academic IELTS with an overall score
  of 6.5, and a letter of motivation.

## Deutschlandstipendium: amount and eligibility

The Deutschlandstipendium pays 300 euros per month: 150 euros from private sponsors and 150 euros from
the federal government. It is awarded for at least two semesters and at most until the end of the
standard period of study.

Who can apply: students of all nationalities enrolled at a campus of SRH University who will still be
enrolled for at least two more semesters after the funding starts. Students in a second or supplementary
degree programme, a Master's programme or a part-time or dual programme can also apply, as can prospective
students waiting for admission. Not eligible: doctoral students, and students at federal or administrative
higher education institutions.

Selection criteria: previous education and academic achievements, letters of recommendation,
extracurricular or social engagement, and the special circumstances of the applicant.

## BAföG

BAföG is state student financing, not a scholarship: half of it is an interest-free loan and the other
half a grant. SRH works with the portal srh.meinbafoeg.de, where an application takes about 30 minutes.

## Student loans and other financing

- KfW student loan: up to 650 euros per month, independent of income.
- DaKa (Berlin): up to 750 euros per month.
- Federal education loan (Bildungskredit): for internships, semesters abroad or studies.
- Income-contingent repayment models: Brain Capital Education Fund, CHANCEN eG (Campus Berlin),
  Deutsche Bildung Study Fund, and FINS for EU Bachelor's and Master's students.
- Independent financing advice: deineStudienfinanzierung.de.
- State programmes (education vouchers by federal state) for people with vocational training who want
  further qualification: https://www.srh-university.de/en/your-studies/financing/national-programmes/
- Child benefit (Kindergeld) can continue until age 25 during studies.
- Student jobs at SRH: srh-karriere.de.

## Discounts and payment

- Alumni discount: SRH graduates receive a 10 percent discount on the total tuition fees of a Master's programme.
- Sibling discount: 10 percent on the tuition fees when a sibling is enrolled at SRH.
- Referral bonus: 500 euros for each successful recommendation of a new student.
- International tuition payments: Flywire, srhuniversity.flywire.com.
