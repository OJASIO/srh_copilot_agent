# Scholarships at SRH University: overview and responsible offices

Source: Student Service hotline FAQ (Stand 31.03.2026), section 8 "Stipendium",
and the public financing page https://www.srh-university.de/en/you-want-to-study/financing/

## General rule

Scholarship advice and questions about the status of a scholarship award are
handled by the responsible office for that scholarship, not by Student Service,
unless the question is general and can be answered from the public financing page.
Different scholarships have different contact persons (see below).

## Career Service & Development

Contact: scholarship.hsg@srh.de

Responsible for:
- Deutschlandstipendium (national merit scholarship, co-funded by the federal government and private sponsors)
- SRH Stipendium für begabte Studierende in besonderen Lebenslagen (SRH scholarship for talented students in special life situations)
- Processing of recommendations and scholarship offers from external providers

## International Office

Contact: international.hsg@srh.de or scholarshipexchange.hsg@srh.de

Responsible for:
- STIBET: for international students already studying in Germany
- Erasmus+: for students going to a European country outside Germany (study or internship)
- PROMOS: for students going to a non-European country outside Germany
- BaWü Stipendium (Baden-Württemberg scholarship): exchange scholarship for a person going abroad and for a person coming to Baden-Württemberg in return
- HAW.International: thesis, research internship abroad, or stay abroad as a doctoral candidate

## Admission

Contact: apply.hsg@srh.de

Responsible for:
- SRH Performance Scholarship. It has many categories and works like a fee reduction for the first year of study.
  Categories: Performance Scholarship, Entrepreneurship Scholarship, Women for Leadership Scholarship,
  Talent Scholarship, Women in Sound Scholarship, Future of Tech Scholarship, Women in Tech Scholarship.
  Applications go through the admission process, so questions about it are answered by Admission.

## Examination Office

Responsible for:
- Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes (German Academic Scholarship Foundation).
  Students with the highest overall GPA are nominated by the Examination Office.
  Students cannot request their own nomination. The deadline is usually 31 July.

## Scholarships NOT supported at SRH

- Hilde Domin Programme: this programme is no longer intended for students of private universities.

## BAföG (state student financing, not a scholarship)

- Which BAföG office is responsible? For first applications: Studierendenwerk Heidelberg. For ongoing applications: the office that handled the application so far. See the BAföG information sheet.
- Who signs the BAföG application? First application (Formblatt 2): Student Service. Applications with transcript of records (Formblatt 5): Examination Office.
- Student Service also helps with BAföG forms as part of its general paperwork support.

## Where to start

For a general overview of financing options (scholarships, BAföG, student loans, part-time work) students are referred to https://www.srh-university.de/en/you-want-to-study/financing/
General questions can go to Student Service: student-service.hsg@srh.de, phone +49 30 515650 200, weekdays 9 am to 3 pm (Fridays until noon).
